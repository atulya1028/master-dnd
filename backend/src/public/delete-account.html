Master DND — Account & Data Deletion

App: Master DND | Developer: Atulya Agrawal

Users of Master DND (operated by Atulya Agrawal) can permanently delete their account and all associated cloud data, or delete individual items (DND profiles, rules, VIP contacts, and session logs) without deleting their account.

Option 1: Delete Data Inside the App

  1. Open Master DND and sign in with your registered email address.
  2. To delete individual items without deleting your account: remove any VIP contact on Home, delete a profile in Profiles, delete a rule in Rules, or delete individual session logs in Stability Report.
  3. To request full account deletion, use Option 2 below or send an email from your registered address.

Option 2: Request Full Account Deletion by Email

  1. Email atulya1992@zohomail.in from the email address registered in Master DND.
  2. Use the subject line "Master DND Account Deletion Request".
  3. We will verify your request and permanently delete your account and associated records within 7 days.

Data Types Deleted vs. Retained

  • Permanently Deleted: User account record (email, name, optional phone number, avatar, bio), saved VIP/emergency contacts, custom DND profiles, automated DND rules, custom preferences, and DND focus/stability activity logs.
  • Retained / Temporary: Transient server access logs (containing no passwords) may be retained for up to 30 days for security and abuse prevention, after which they are automatically purged.
