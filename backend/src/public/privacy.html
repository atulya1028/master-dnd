Master DND — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 23, 2026 | Last Updated: November 2026

At Master DND ("we", "our", or "us"), operated by Atulya Agrawal, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application and cloud backend services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Authentication Information: When you sign in to Master DND, we collect your email address solely to send a temporary 6-digit One-Time Password (OTP) via Zoho Mail SMTP. We do not require passwords or social network sign-ins.
  • Profile Data: User-customizable display name, avatar initial, and profile metadata (stored to sync your quiet time configurations).
  • Local Rules & VIP Contacts: DND schedules, active hours, and user-specified allowed VIP contacts are stored securely on your local device using Android SharedPreferences.
  • No Location or Audio Tracking: We do NOT collect, record, or transmit your voice calls, SMS messages, audio recordings, or real-time GPS locations.

2. App Permissions & Why We Need Them

  • ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY: Allows Master DND to toggle Do Not Disturb mode, filter priority notifications, and mute interruptions according to your scheduled quiet times.
  • MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS: Used to adjust ringer, media, and alarm sound levels when custom quiet profiles activate.
  • INTERNET: Enables communication with our secure Microsoft Azure API endpoint for authentication and profile sync.

3. Security & Data Protection

All data transmitted between the Master DND mobile application and our Microsoft Azure App Service backend is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). JSON Web Tokens (JWT) are cryptographically signed and stored in private device storage.

4. Third-Party Services

We work only with reputable, enterprise-grade cloud providers:

  • Microsoft Azure: Backend hosting and cloud computing (Central India region).
  • MongoDB Atlas: Encrypted cloud database for user preferences.
  • Zoho Mail: Transactional SMTP email delivery for one-time verification codes.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete your account, saved rules, and preferences at any time directly inside the app under Profile Settings or by emailing us. Upon request, all associated account data is permanently deleted from our servers.
